The Android app is in a closed beta on Google Play. I need about 20 testers for two weeks before Google will let me release it. If a Pro version is ever released, beta testers get it free.

**You need:** an Android phone with Garmin Connect, paired with a [supported Garmin watch](https://apps.garmin.com/apps/f7252e60-09aa-40d2-87da-7fc731324aa9), and GeoBallistics (Comp or Chart) or Applied Ballistics.

## Android: join in 5 steps

1. **Ask to join the beta group:** [groups.google.com/g/dopesync-beta](https://groups.google.com/g/dopesync-beta). Use the same Google account as your phone's Play Store. In the request message, add your watch and phone model. I approve requests within a day.
2. **Once approved, open the test link** on your phone and tap **Become a tester**: [play.google.com/apps/testing/com.taylormadetools.dopesync](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.taylormadetools.dopesync)
3. **Install DOPE Sync** from the Play Store link on that page. It can take a few minutes after step 2 to show up.
4. **Install the watch app** from the [Connect IQ Store](https://apps.garmin.com/apps/f7252e60-09aa-40d2-87da-7fc731324aa9), then **open DOPE Sync on the watch once**. Your first share won't reach the watch until you do.
5. **Share a card:** in your ballistic app, Export, CSV, Share, then tap DOPE Sync. See the [user guide]({{ site.baseurl }}/user-guide/) for each app's steps.

**During the beta:** everything is unlocked. Please use the **Send feedback** button in the app (or the [feedback form](https://forms.gle/3sWLNwVvi64kEqgDA)) at least once, even if it's just "works on my fenix 7". Google asks how the test went, and your feedback is the answer. Please stay in the beta for the full two weeks.

## iPhone

The iPhone app is built and working, and it's next once Android is out. Two things you can do today:

- **Use DOPE Sync now:** install the watch app and paste your card into its settings in Garmin Connect. No phone app needed. Steps are in the [user guide]({{ site.baseurl }}/user-guide/).
- **Join the iPhone waitlist:** [sign up here](https://forms.gle/2kExpoTEvotdtx3q9) (one minute, no sign-in). I'll email you when it's ready, or earlier if you want to test it.
