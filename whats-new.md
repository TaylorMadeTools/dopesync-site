## 0.3.7 (October 2026)
- Feedback link in the watch settings (bugs, ideas, or request another ballistic app)

## 0.3.6 (October 2026)
- Paste a card works with text copied from Google Drive or a file preview, not just the raw CSV

## 0.3.5 (October 2026)
- Clearer tactical mode labels in settings and the watch menu
- "No card yet" message fits on every watch screen

## 0.3.4 (October 2026)
- Paste a card in the watch settings, no phone app needed (GeoBallistics or Applied Ballistics CSV text)
- "On this watch" in settings lists your stage card and pins

## 0.3.3 (October 2026)
- Tactical mode is black and red only across the whole card
- Hold Start on the card to switch tactical mode on or off (also in the menu)

## 0.3.2 (October 2026)
- Cards still send if you leave the share screen right away (Android)
- Shorter, cleaner "open" prompt on the watch

## 0.3.1 (October 2026)
- The watch confirms a new card right away, even if you don't open the app
- No more false "Watch didn't confirm" message

## 0.3.0 (October 2026)
- Supports 62 Garmin watch models: fenix 5 Plus through fenix 9 and E, epix, Enduro, MARQ, Forerunner 245 to 970
- Card layout adapts to each screen size
- GeoBallistics Chart view exports (full trajectory tables)
- Two-wind ranges always readable

## 0.2.0 (October 2026)
- Applied Ballistics CSV support (numbered or lettered targets, two-wind ranges)
- Rows your ballistic app didn't solve show `--` instead of a misleading `0.0`
- Wind clock position for both apps
- New app icon, About screen

## 0.1.0 (October 2026)
- First beta: GeoBallistics stage cards from Android to fenix 6 Pro
- Stage card + 5 pins, colored hold letters, wind clock position
