Range card CSV files to try DOPE Sync without building your own card first. Download one on your phone, then share it to **DOPE Sync** (Android), or open it and paste its text into the watch settings.

| File | From |
|---|---|
| [geoballistics-comp-sample.csv]({{ site.baseurl }}/samples/geoballistics-comp-sample.csv) | GeoBallistics Comp card, 7 targets, MRAD, yards |
| [geoballistics-chart-sample.csv]({{ site.baseurl }}/samples/geoballistics-chart-sample.csv) | GeoBallistics Chart, 18 ranges, MRAD, yards |
| [applied-ballistics-sample.csv]({{ site.baseurl }}/samples/applied-ballistics-sample.csv) | Applied Ballistics, 6 targets, two-wind holds |

These are example numbers, not dope for your rifle.
